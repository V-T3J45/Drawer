Now select Linear inside the Annotation ( center top ), now click on the first two lines on the left top ( as shown in the image), move the mouse left side a bit, and left click again

Now repeat this for all the lines make sure your start line should be the top line and your selection should go like this (1,3) (1,4) (1,5) (1,6) ... 

Now you would have all the dimension on the left

Now on the bottom click left bottom and click 2nd line on the bottom towards right, move the cursor downwards and left click again, now you will have the dimension showing 40, repeat this for the other two lines.

Your Drawing is complete.
