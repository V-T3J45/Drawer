Select line tool, Turn on Ortho mode, on the leftmost connect all the lines ( start from left top to bottom).

Now you are at the bottom, now move mouse right side, type 40, press enter, then connect to the topmost line.

Now you are at the top, move the mouse to the right side, type 80, press enter, then connect to the bottom most line.

Now you at the the bottom , now press escape, connect the right most side of the table using line tool.

Now you have a table like structure.
