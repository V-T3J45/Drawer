1. Turn on Ortho mode, Select line tool (left top), Left click, move mouse towards right, and type 170 and press enter. ( Now you have one line on the screen).

2. Next in the command box type offset, press Enter, and type 10 and press enter, left click on the line , move mouse downward and left click, ( now you have 2 lines).

3. On the 2nd line left click, mov cursor downward and left click, repeat this until you have 7 lines.
